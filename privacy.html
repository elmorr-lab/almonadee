AL-MONADEE · المنادي

Privacy Policy — effective 4 November 2026

AL-MONADEE (المنادي) is free software published on Google Play by elmorr (the developer, “we”), as a contribution from EJAD Gulf Technologies Co. Ltd., Al Khobar, Saudi Arabia, with its permission. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and neither the developer nor EJAD operates a server or receives any data from the app.

1. DATA WE COLLECT

None. We run no servers for this app and have no analytics, no advertising network, no crash reporting and no user accounts. We cannot identify you and we receive nothing from your device.

2. WHAT THE APP SENDS, AND TO WHOM

To do its job the app talks directly to a small number of public services from your device:

a) Prayer times — Aladhan API (Islamic Network). The app sends the coordinates of the location you selected, the calculation method and the month, and receives that month's prayer times. No identifier of you or your device is attached.

b) Finding your location (only when you press "Detect my location", or when you accept the suggestion on first run) — ipwho.is, with ipapi.co as a fallback. These services see your internet address and return an approximate city. The app does not send your address anywhere else, and the app never uses GPS.

c) City search (only when you type a city name) — Open-Meteo geocoding. The app sends the text you typed and receives matching places.

d) Qur'an text and recitation (only when those features are on) — Al Quran Cloud API and the Islamic Network CDN. The app sends the verse reference, the translation edition and the reciter you chose.

e) Athan recordings (only when you download a voice) — the Islamic Network CDN.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy and will see your internet address as any website does. The app sends them nothing about you beyond what is described above.

3. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your settings, the downloaded prayer-times calendar, cached verses, downloaded Athan recordings and any photos you choose as backgrounds are stored on the device only. Uninstalling the app removes them.

4. PERMISSIONS AND WHY

• Internet and network state — to download prayer times, verses and recordings, and to show whether the device is online.
• Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) — to call the Athan at the exact minute the prayer time enters. This is the core purpose of the app.
• Display over other apps — to show the prayer banner over whatever is on screen at prayer time. Optional; the app works without it.
• Notifications — to show the ongoing notice while the Athan is playing.
• Run at startup — only if you turn on "Keep working after the TV restarts", so the Athan still sounds after a power cut.
• Photos — the app uses the system photo picker. It can read only the photos you pick, never your library.

5. CHILDREN

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. YOUR CHOICES

Every feature that sends anything can be switched off in Settings: the Qur'an panel and recitation, the voice downloads, and location detection (choose your city from the built-in list instead). Prayer times themselves require one request per month per location.
