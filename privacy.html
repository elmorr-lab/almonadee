AL-MONADEE · المنادي

Privacy Policy — effective 8 November 2026 (version 6.0 and later)

AL-MONADEE (المنادي) is free software published on Google Play by elmorr (the developer, “we”), as a contribution from EJAD Gulf Technologies Co. Ltd., Al Khobar, Saudi Arabia, with its permission. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and neither the developer nor EJAD operates a server or receives any data from the app.

1. DATA WE COLLECT

None. We run no servers for this app and have no analytics, no advertising network, no crash reporting and no user accounts. We cannot identify you and we receive nothing from your device.

2. WHAT THE APP SENDS, AND TO WHOM

Prayer times are calculated on your device, from the city you chose. Your location is never sent anywhere for that. (Versions 5.2 and earlier downloaded the month's times from the Aladhan API, which received the coordinates of the chosen city; version 6.0 ended this.)

To do the rest of its job the app talks directly to a small number of public services from your device, only for the features named:

a) Clock check — the app asks a public server (api.quran.com) for the current time once in a while, to warn you if the device clock is wrong. Nothing about you is sent.

b) Finding your location (only when you press "Detect my location", or when you accept the suggestion on first run) — ipwho.is, with ipapi.co as a fallback. These services see your internet address and return an approximate city. The app does not send your address anywhere else, and the app never uses GPS.

c) City search (only when you type a city name) — Open-Meteo geocoding. The app sends the text you typed and receives matching places.

d) Qur'an text, translations and recitation (only when those features are on) — Quran.com (Quran Foundation): api.quran.com and its audio servers. The app sends the verse or surah reference, the translation and the reciter you chose.

f) Hadith — none. The short list of sahih hadith shown in the app is bundled inside it; nothing is fetched. The "View source" link opens dorar.net in your browser only when you tap it.

e) Athan recordings (only when you download a voice) — the Islamic Network CDN.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy and will see your internet address as any website does. The app sends them nothing about you beyond what is described above.

3. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your settings, the calculated prayer times, cached verses, downloaded Athan recordings, your reading position in the Qur'an and any photos you choose as backgrounds are stored on the device only. Home-screen widgets read the same local data. Uninstalling the app removes them.

4. PERMISSIONS AND WHY

• Internet and network state — to download prayer times, verses and recordings, and to show whether the device is online.
• Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) — to call the Athan at the exact minute the prayer time enters. This is the core purpose of the app.
• Display over other apps — to show the prayer banner over whatever is on screen at prayer time. Optional; the app works without it.
• Notifications — the countdown before each prayer, the notice while the Athan plays, the message when the phone is on silent, and the quiet dua after the Athan.
• Full-screen notifications (phones) — to show the Athan screen over the lock screen at prayer time, like an alarm clock. Optional.
• Ignore battery optimisations (phones) — only if you allow it, so the system does not delay the Athan. Optional.
• Run at startup — only if you turn on "Keep working after the TV restarts", so the Athan still sounds after a power cut.
• Photos — the app uses the system photo picker. It can read only the photos you pick, never your library.

5. CHILDREN

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. YOUR CHOICES

Every feature that sends anything can be switched off in Settings: the Qur'an verse and recitation, the voice downloads, and location detection (choose your city from the built-in list instead). Prayer times and the Athan work entirely offline.
