AL-MONADEE · المنادي

Prayer times, Athan, Qibla, the Qur'an page by page, athkar and sahih hadith — for iPhone, iPad and Android. Free: no account, no ads, no tracking.

SUPPORT

Questions, a fault, or a mistake in a time or a text? Write to elmorrdev@gmail.com — say which device you use and the city chosen in the app. In the app: Settings → About → Reach out.

للأسئلة أو للإبلاغ عن خطأ في وقت أو نص: elmorrdev@gmail.com — واذكر جهازك والمدينة المختارة في التطبيق. ومن التطبيق: الإعدادات ← حول التطبيق ← تواصل معنا.

IF THE ATHAN DOES NOT RING

In the app: Settings → Athan & alerts → “If the Athan does not ring” shows what your device needs, and “Test the Athan in 1 minute” lets you hear it.
